Information Systems

Build a career at the intersection of business, technology, and people.

Information Systems (IS) is the field where business problems meet digital tools. IS professionals design systems, analyze data, secure information, and create experiences that help organizations run smarter and serve people better.

Discover more

What is Information Systems?

Information Systems is a broad career field focused on using technology to improve how organizations operate. It includes everything from managing databases and analyzing trends, to developing software, protecting company systems, and improving the digital experiences people use every day.

Business + Tech

IS professionals solve real business challenges using technology, processes, and strategy.

Problem Solving

They connect data, tools, and user needs to improve operations and decision-making.

People + Systems

IS work is about making systems easier for people to understand, use, and trust.

Career Variety

There are paths in data, cybersecurity, software, design, and many other business-facing roles.

Behavioral Interview Practice

Use the STAR method to structure clear and specific answers to behavioral interview questions.

The STAR method

S

Situation

Briefly describe the context or background.

T

Task

Explain your responsibility or what needed to be accomplished.

A

Action

Describe the specific actions YOU took.

R

Result

Explain the outcome and what you learned.
